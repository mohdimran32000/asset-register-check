How it works. Plain rules, no AI. They look for duplicates, empty fields, the same thing spelled several ways, dates that can't be right, parents that don't exist, and floors missing assets their neighbours have.
The score is the share of assets with no problem found, counting assets a floor seems to be missing. It's this tool's own rubric, not an industry standard.
The samples are fictional, generated from the rules of the Procedural Dubai tower at 80 floors. Generic vendors, made-up serials. The messy one has 190 problems planted on purpose, listed in an answer key.
